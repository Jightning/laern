# Syllabus — DEMO 001

Not an institutional course. The sequence is ordered by who needs it: the first
section is everything a reader must know to use the site, and the rest is why it
works that way and what it can render.

## Course-level features

`course.yaml`: `valueStyles`, custom `styles`, `syntax` (comment / keywords /
patterns / strings), `theme.hue`, `state.enabled`. `blocks.js`: the `metric`
custom renderer. Generated: `problems.md`, `checklist.md`. See also
[schedule](#/demo/) and [reference](#/demo/).
